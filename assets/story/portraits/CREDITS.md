# Photographic newspaper portraits

These are real photographic portraits, converted to greyscale and cropped for
small printed atlas islands. They are not AI portraits, banknote substitutions,
or the former engraving illustrations.

- **Sigmund Freud**: Max Halberstadt, c.1921 photograph. Retrieved from the
  reproduction shown at https://www.meisterdrucke.us/fine-art-prints/Max-Halberstadt/1636828/Portrait-of-Sigmund-Freud-in-1921.html .
- **Carl Rogers**: photograph accompanying Cynthia Vinney's biographical article
  at https://www.thoughtco.com/carl-rogers-4588296 . This source's reuse rights
  have not been independently verified; do not describe the photograph as
  public-domain or freely licensed without checking its rights holder.
- **William James**: photographic portrait reproduced on
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_James (Wikimedia file
  `William_James_b1842c.jpg`).

The six newspaper and eight landscape UV islands are derived from the supplied
scene atlases. Headings are **editorial summaries**, not attributed verbatim
quotations or historical newspaper facsimiles. Portrait placement is baked at
build time; the running scene makes no remote requests for these photographs.
The supplied original burning-banknote KTX2 is untouched.
