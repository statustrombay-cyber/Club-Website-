The Atrium

Ten rooms of the mind, one room to meet in

Scroll to enter
i — The Room

A quiet room,
open to anyone

Mindful Tattva is a psychology club. We keep one long room with ten faces on its walls, a bench to sit on, and no hurry at all. Come in, stay as long as you like.

ii — Conversations

Small circles,
honest talking

Fortnightly, eight or ten of us sit down with a question. No lectern, no jargon — only the plain work of saying what is true and hearing it back.

  • What we discuss
iii — The Reading Room

Texts we
return to

A short shelf: Freud on the everyday, Jung on the image, Bowlby on the bond, Frankl on meaning. We read one essay a month and leave the margins for each other.

  • About the club
iv — The Wall

Ten faces
who gave us words

Portraits line the atrium: the people whose language we borrow when we try to describe a mind. Later this wall becomes ours — photographs from the club, in their place.

  • The club wall
v — The Field

Then the room
opens outward

Beyond the last arch the floor gives way to dunes. Everything we talk about indoors is meant to travel — into work, into families, into the ordinary afternoon.

vi — Visit

Come and sit
with us

We meet in Bombay, in person, usually on a Saturday afternoon. Write to us and we will send the address and the next reading.
